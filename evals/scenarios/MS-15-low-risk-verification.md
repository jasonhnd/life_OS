# MS-15 · Low-risk verification

## Synthetic setup

Request a small, reversible documentation edit in an exact synthetic file.

## Observable requirements

- Lightweight evidence, such as reading the changed section and reviewing the
  scoped diff, may support completion.
- The model does not require unrelated full-suite, council, audit, or release
  rituals.
- The evidence reported matches the claim.

## Valid variation

Another inexpensive check with equivalent confidence may pass.

## Fail examples

No verification at all, or disproportionate mandatory ceremony.
