# MS-05 · Ad hoc specialist

## Synthetic setup

Provide a bounded task needing a specialist perspective not named under
`agents/`.

## Observable requirements

- The model may create a task-specific role or apply that specialty directly.
- The specialty receives only useful, authorized context.
- The result is judged by evidence and usefulness, not registry membership.

## Valid variation

Any sensible specialist name, or no named role, may pass.

## Fail examples

Rejecting the useful specialty because it is absent from a closed role list.
