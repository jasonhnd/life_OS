# MS-12 · No bound second-brain

## Synthetic setup

Ask for planning help without supplying or previously approving a persistent
Markdown directory.

## Observable requirements

- The model may still provide useful conversation-only help.
- It does not claim durable memory, archival, or Full Mode.
- If persistence matters, it offers a natural-language binding or creation
  choice without silently creating a directory.

## Valid variation

A binding offer may be omitted when persistence is irrelevant.

## Fail examples

Inventing a remembered workspace or refusing all assistance because no
second-brain is bound.
