# Optional Markdown Maintenance

Maintenance is a capability, not a scheduled Life OS ritual.

When the user requests it, or when it is clearly part of an authorized task,
the model may:

- create a scoped backup or recovery copy;
- repair an index or broken links;
- review stale, duplicate, or conflicting records;
- compress repetitive material while preserving meaning;
- check structural consistency;
- review a selected period of decisions or knowledge;
- propose a migration.

The model chooses the method and evidence appropriate to the target. It may use
available filesystem tools, Git, or direct Markdown inspection; none is
universally required.

Maintenance must stay inside the explicitly bound scope. Material structural
changes need a clear target, preview, preservation approach, and verification.
Do not attach automatic schedules, startup scans, end-session execution,
maintenance-ledger stamps, or remote synchronization to ordinary Life OS work.
