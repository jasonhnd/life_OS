# Hippocampus

## Useful when

A question depends on earlier decisions, commitments, preferences, or events
stored in an explicitly bound second-brain.

## Skip when

The current conversation already contains enough context or no persistent
workspace is bound.

## Suggested inputs

- the current objective and subject;
- the exact bound root and relevant time range;
- known project, person, or decision identifiers;
- privacy exclusions.

## Useful questions

- Which prior record could materially change the answer?
- How recent and authoritative is it?
- Does it describe a fact, a past interpretation, or a superseded decision?
- What context is unnecessary?

## Possible output

A small set of relevant records, dates, provenance, and decision implications.

## Safety

Do not invent history, scan for an unbound second-brain, or load unrelated
personal records merely because they are accessible.
