# Finance

## Useful when

Money, assets, cash flow, financing, taxes, pricing, or financial risk
materially affects the choice.

## Skip when

Financial impact is immaterial or the task needs only a clearly supplied
arithmetic transformation.

## Suggested inputs

- amounts, units, currencies, and timing;
- source data and confidence;
- constraints, alternatives, and downside tolerance;
- applicable jurisdiction or account context when relevant.

## Useful questions

- Which assumptions drive the result?
- What happens under a realistic downside case?
- How do liquidity and opportunity cost change the choice?
- Which fact needs fresher or qualified evidence?

## Possible output

A calculation, cash-flow view, sensitivity table, trade-off analysis, or
conditional recommendation with assumptions.

## Safety

Distinguish facts from estimates and use current evidence when accuracy is high
stakes. Do not present general analysis as individualized regulated financial
or tax advice.
