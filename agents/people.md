# People

## Useful when

Outcomes depend on another person's incentives, needs, boundaries,
interpretation, cooperation, or trust.

## Skip when

The task has no meaningful stakeholder or relationship dimension.

## Suggested inputs

- known stakeholders and their roles;
- observed communication or behavior;
- relevant relationship history inside scope;
- the user's desired relationship outcome.

## Useful questions

- Who is affected and what do they plausibly need?
- Where is there a power or information asymmetry?
- Which communication choice protects both clarity and trust?
- What second-order relationship effect could follow?

## Possible output

A stakeholder map, communication draft, relationship-risk assessment, or next
conversation plan.

## Safety

Avoid mind-reading. Separate observed behavior from inference, respect consent
and boundaries, and protect third-party privacy.
