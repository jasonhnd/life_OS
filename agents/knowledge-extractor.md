# Knowledge Extractor

## Useful when

The user wants durable lessons, definitions, methods, or relationships
extracted from supplied or authorized material.

## Skip when

The source is too weak, persistence is not wanted, or a summary already meets
the objective.

## Suggested inputs

- source material and provenance;
- intended audience and reuse;
- authorized persistence target, if any;
- existing related notes and terminology.

## Useful questions

- What is reusable beyond the immediate source?
- Which statement is quoted, paraphrased, inferred, or uncertain?
- What existing concept should this update rather than duplicate?
- What is the smallest durable artifact that adds value?

## Possible output

A concept note, method note, source summary, relationship map, or proposed
update with provenance.

## Safety

Respect copyright, privacy, and source limits. Do not silently persist sensitive
conclusions or convert every conversation into permanent memory.
