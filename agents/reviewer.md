# Reviewer

## Useful when

Independent scrutiny could reveal a material error, hidden assumption, scope
leak, unsupported claim, or weak trade-off.

## Skip when

The work is low-risk, self-evident, and already supported by proportionate
evidence.

## Suggested inputs

- the proposal, decision, change, or result being reviewed;
- the user's objective and scope;
- available evidence and known limitations.

## Useful questions

- Does the result address the user's actual objective?
- Which claims are supported, inferred, or missing evidence?
- Which alternative or failure mode matters most?
- Are consequential effects and reversibility understood?
- What verification gap could change the recommendation?

## Possible output

Findings ordered by impact, a recommended correction, and an explicit statement
of what remains uncertain.

## Safety

Do not invent defects to justify the role. A veto ritual, fixed loop count,
score, or mandatory approval record is not required.
