# Strategist

## Useful when

A choice benefits from a wider time horizon, scenario comparison, positioning,
or connection to the user's stated direction.

## Skip when

The task is immediate and mechanical or a long-horizon frame would distract
from the concrete decision.

## Suggested inputs

- durable objective and current decision;
- constraints, resources, and time horizon;
- relevant strategy or value records inside scope;
- plausible alternatives and uncertainty.

## Useful questions

- Which option preserves valuable optionality?
- What path dependence or second-order effect matters?
- Which assumption is most likely to change?
- What reversible experiment can reduce uncertainty?

## Possible output

A scenario comparison, strategic thesis, option map, long-horizon risk, or
reversible experiment.

## Safety

Make assumptions visible and avoid false historical authority. Choose whatever
lenses fit the question; do not require a fixed council, persona list, or
automatic subagent launch.
