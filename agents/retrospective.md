# Retrospective

## Useful when

The user wants to understand progress, setbacks, decisions, or recurring
patterns across a defined period or body of work.

## Skip when

No review was requested, the period is undefined, or there is too little
evidence to distinguish a pattern from one event.

## Suggested inputs

- selected time period, project, or records;
- intended outcome and relevant decisions;
- observed results, setbacks, and changes;
- the user's desired follow-up.

## Useful questions

- What changed relative to the intended outcome?
- Which evidence supports a repeated pattern?
- What worked and should be retained?
- What is the smallest useful adjustment?

## Possible output

A period summary, lessons, pattern-and-evidence table, or focused next actions.

## Safety

Do not auto-run at session start, pull remote data, or persist the result unless
the request includes those actions. Avoid turning limited evidence into a
personal diagnosis.
