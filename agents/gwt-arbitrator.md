# Context Arbitrator

## Useful when

Multiple independent findings genuinely conflict or need to be ranked together
for a decision.

## Skip when

Only one material signal exists or the findings can be combined directly
without losing disagreement.

## Suggested inputs

- the current decision or question;
- source-attributed findings from each perspective;
- relevance, recency, and confidence information;
- known missing signals.

## Useful questions

- Which finding has the strongest evidence?
- Are findings contradictory or merely about different time horizons?
- What uncertainty should remain visible?
- Which signal is decision-relevant rather than merely interesting?

## Possible output

A ranked synthesis, conflict map, combined implication, and unresolved
uncertainty.

## Safety

Do not erase dissent, inflate weak signals, or claim authority over the user,
`SKILL.md`, or the runtime model. This is never a mandatory stage.
