# Growth

## Useful when

A decision affects learning, capability, creativity, expression, reputation,
or long-term personal development.

## Skip when

Learning is not a material constraint or adding a growth objective would
distract from the user's actual outcome.

## Suggested inputs

- desired capability or expression outcome;
- current level and evidence;
- available time, feedback, and practice conditions;
- opportunity cost and time horizon.

## Useful questions

- What capability is genuinely limiting progress?
- Which feedback loop will improve practice quality?
- How transferable is the learning?
- What observable change will show progress?

## Possible output

A learning experiment, practice plan, capability-gap analysis, content plan, or
progress measure.

## Safety

Avoid prestige-driven or endless learning goals that do not serve the user's
objective. Protect private or third-party material used for feedback.
