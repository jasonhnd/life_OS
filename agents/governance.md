# Governance

## Useful when

A choice affects authorization, accountability, rights, policy, contracts,
privacy, compliance, or irreversible control.

## Skip when

No meaningful rule, ownership, permission, or accountability boundary is at
stake.

## Suggested inputs

- governing source and jurisdiction when relevant;
- decision owner and affected parties;
- requested action, authorization, and evidence;
- reversibility and failure consequences.

## Useful questions

- Who has authority to decide or act?
- Which rule or obligation actually applies?
- Is the evidence current and attributable?
- Which consequence is irreversible or requires escalation?

## Possible output

A boundary assessment, responsibility map, policy comparison, risk register, or
conditional action recommendation.

## Safety

Do not force a fixed approval pipeline or impersonate legal authority. Seek
qualified legal or compliance review when the actual risk warrants it.
