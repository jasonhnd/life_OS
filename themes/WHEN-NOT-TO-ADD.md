# Theme Boundary

Themes are optional presentation adapters. They may suggest display names,
language, emoji, headings, and tone. They never change:

- what the model may do;
- which agents, tools, or steps must be used;
- authorization, privacy, persistence, or verification;
- the meaning of natural-language requests;
- the user's ability to change language or tone at any time.

Add a theme only when a real audience benefits from a coherent presentation
vocabulary. It may map as many roles as useful; unlisted roles keep natural
names. Do not add trigger tables, command aliases, workflow rules, or duplicated
agent behavior.
