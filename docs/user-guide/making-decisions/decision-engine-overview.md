# Dynamic Decision Support

Life OS does not run every decision through one engine sequence.

For a small choice, the model may answer directly. For a complex or high-impact
choice, it may clarify the objective, inspect relevant records, compare
scenarios, use domain perspectives, seek independent review, or test
assumptions. The exact combination depends on the decision.

Useful lenses can include:

- people and relationships;
- money and resources;
- learning and growth;
- execution and timing;
- governance and downside;
- health and infrastructure;
- values, identity, and long-term direction.

These lenses are options, not six mandatory reports.

A strong decision result normally makes its evidence, assumptions, trade-offs,
uncertainty, recommendation, and next action clear. High-risk decisions receive
greater scrutiny, but not a ceremonial agent chain.
